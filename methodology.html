Your gut has a score. Here's exactly how it's computed.

Some of Flora's scores will not be what you were told — steamed broccoli scores 39, ribeye scores 90. This page is the model in plain language, plus all 100 scores from our public scorecard, unedited. Argue with any of them; that's what the page is for.

The model, in plain language

Flora scores every food and meal 0–100 for one question only: how much work does your gut have to do to deal with this? Not calories. Not fat grams. Not food morality.

🥩

Foods your gut recognizes score high

Ruminant meat, eggs, fish, ripe fruit, roots, raw honey, fermented and whole dairy — single-ingredient foods humans have digested for millennia start at the top of the scale. There's simply nothing in them to fight.

🏭

Industrial food scores low

Seed oils, refined sugar, refined flour, additives, dyes and ultra-processed formulations pull scores down hard — with seed oils weighted as the single worst offender. Two "identical" products can sit 40 points apart on their ingredient panels alone.

💨

Fermentable load counts — even in "health" foods

Cruciferous veg, legumes and soy are high-FODMAP: they ferment in the gut and are among the most common causes of gas and bloating. That's why steamed broccoli starts at 39. It isn't "junk food" — it's honest accounting of digestive work.

🧬

Then YOUR body personalizes it

Base scores reflect how often a food causes gut trouble in general. Report how you actually feel and Flora builds your personal trigger map — the same meal scores differently for you than for anyone else, based on your body's real responses, never on assumptions.

📈

Two scores that mean something

Today's Eating is the honest average of what you ate today. Your Gut Health score is the long game — it moves a few points a day at most, climbs with consistent good days, and heals overnight while you sleep. No single meal ruins you; no single salad saves you.

🔬

Backed, corrected, never fudged

Every score traces to a stated mechanism, and the in-app Gut Science library carries real published studies explained in plain language. Think a score is wrong? Every ingredient has a "Score seem off?" flag — corrections feed the engine. Scores are never edited for marketing.

Where we break from mainstream ratings

Most food scores grade calories, saturated fat, or plant-versus-animal morality — so they call steak a sin and wave broccoli through. Flora grades digestive reality. That inverts some famous verdicts:

🥦

39

🥩

If broccoli never bothers you, great — log it, report how you feel, and your personal trigger map reflects that. The 39 is the starting point for a gut Flora hasn't met yet, not a verdict on yours.

Honesty rules

What these scores are — and aren't

  • Every number is the engine's real output. The scores above were generated by the same engine that ships in the app, and this page is regenerated from it — no score is ever hand-edited to make better content.
  • Base scores are starting points, not verdicts. They reflect how often a food causes gut trouble in general. Your reports teach Flora which of those risks actually apply to you — that personalization is the whole product.
  • Neutral means neutral. Where the engine has no strong signal, it says so and scores 50 — it doesn't invent an opinion.
  • Disagreement is welcome, with receipts. Every ingredient in the app carries its mechanism and, where we've curated one, a published study. If you think a score is wrong, flag it in-app — corrections feed the engine.
  • Flora offers general wellness information, not medical advice. If you have a diagnosed condition, work with your clinician — Flora is a scoreboard, not a doctor.
