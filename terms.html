Flora is not a medical device and provides no medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Scores, projections, trigger analyses, and recommendations are informational estimates built from general nutrition research and your own logged data. If you have a medical condition, food allergy, or eating disorder, talk to your doctor before acting on Flora's suggestions.

4. Flora accounts

A Flora account is optional — everything in Flora works without one. If you create one, it is with Sign in with Apple, Google, or your email address: the provider performs the sign in (for email, Flora sends a one-time code to your address and there is never a password), and Flora keeps the identifier the provider assigns to it, plus your name and email address where the provider shares them. Creating an account (or signing in to one) turns on cloud backup: Flora keeps one copy of your Flora data — your profile, meals and scores, learned triggers, check-ins, saved recipes, grocery list and reminder settings — on Flora's own server under your account, so a new phone can restore it with one sign in. You can turn backup off at any time (You → Privacy & data) and delete the server copy from the same screen; with it off, nothing is uploaded. Without an account, cloud backup stays off unless you turn it on yourself. What the backup contains and how it is stored is described in the Privacy Policy. Your account is not your subscription: Flora Plus stays with your Apple ID, and signing out does not affect it. A web access code works differently: it is tied to the phone you redeem it on, not to your Apple ID.

You can delete your account inside the app at any time (You → Account → Delete account). Deletion erases the account, its sign-in sessions and its cloud backup from Flora's server (and revokes Flora's Sign in with Apple authorization for an Apple account), and then erases everything on the phone. There is no undo. Deleting your account does not cancel a Flora Plus subscription — cancel that separately in your iPhone's Settings (your name → Subscriptions) or in the App Store, or it will keep renewing.
