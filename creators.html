What to post

The format that works is the scan-reaction: point the camera at real food, scan it on screen, react to the number. The score does the arguing for you. Three shapes of it:

1. The cereal test. Scan the food diet culture worships, then the food it fears — a "whole grain" cereal, then a ribeye — side by side, and react to 45 vs 93 on camera. The comment section finishes the video for you.

2. Scan your actual fridge. Walk your kitchen and scan five staples you already eat and defend — liver, butter, raw honey, sardines. "An app finally agrees with me" is the whole arc.

3. Score my food. Ask followers to name foods in the comments, scan the most-argued ones in the next video. Repeatable weekly, and the requests tell you exactly what your audience wants settled.
